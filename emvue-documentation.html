Case Study

Reinventing Docs for an ENMS

A ground-up documentation project for EmVue, an energy management product, covering content strategy, stakeholder collaboration, QA, versioning, and publication.

A new product needed a complete documentation system from day one.

The EmVue documentation suite was planned, written, reviewed, versioned, and published from scratch to support product adoption across versions 1.0 to 1.2.

Client
PcVue
Product
EmVue
Role
Technical Content Developer
Published
April 23, 2025

Context

EmVue was a new product in PcVue's portfolio, designed to provide information that meets the needs of energy experts. Because the product was new, there was no existing documentation framework to build on.

This created an opportunity to design a documentation strategy from the ground up, aligned with product development milestones, user needs, and internal stakeholder expectations.

Skills & Expertise

  • Documentation strategy & planning
  • Technical writing & topic-based authoring
  • Subject matter expert interviews
  • Stakeholder review & quality assurance
  • MadCap Flare & Git
  • Release-aligned content management

Process

01

Research & Information Gathering

I reviewed product specifications and design documents, then interviewed SMEs to gather accurate technical information.

02

Content Development

I created clear, concise documentation types tailored to the target audience, including tutorials, quickstarts, concepts, references, and FAQs.

03

Feedback & QA

I established a stakeholder feedback loop to review drafts, clarify details, and improve accuracy, consistency, and usability.

04

Versioning & Publication

I managed updates for versions 1.0, 1.1, and 1.2, using MadCap Flare and Git to publish documentation to the PcVue docs portal.

My Role

  • Developed the documentation plan and strategy
  • Collaborated with developers, product managers, and QA teams
  • Conducted documentation reviews and quality assurance
  • Managed documentation changes across product versions
  • Published content through MadCap Flare

Solution

The documentation was built as a structured suite that could support both end-users and internal teams. It provided product concepts, procedural guidance, reference information, and in-app help support for users working inside the application context.

Outcomes

EmVue gained a documentation suite supporting its first product releases:

  • Delivered documentation for versions 1.0, 1.1, and 1.2.
  • Created tutorials, quickstarts, concepts, references, and FAQs for users and internal teams.
  • Provided content supporting in-app help.
  • Established a reviewed, version-controlled content foundation for future updates.

What Changed

  • Documentation strategy existed from the first product versions
  • Content was aligned with product release milestones
  • Stakeholder review became part of the workflow
  • Users gained support inside and around the product experience
