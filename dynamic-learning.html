Case Study

From PDFs to Dynamic Learning

A training transformation project that converted static PDF materials into modular, interactive, feedback-enabled e-learning content.

Long static PDFs became a modular learning system with web and LMS outputs.

The project restructured MadCap Flare content into topic-based modules, added navigation and exercises, and prepared training for SCORM delivery, localization, and continuous feedback.

Client
PcVue
Type
E-learning Transformation
Role
LX Dev
Published
April 23, 2025

Context

The company's training materials were previously distributed as lengthy, static PDFs. They lacked interactivity, contextual navigation, progress tracking, and feedback mechanisms.

Learners struggled to engage with the material, while the company had limited visibility into training effectiveness or opportunities for improvement.

Key Features

  • Modular content delivery with exercises and improved navigation
  • Dual output for LMS and web formats
  • SCORM-compliant output for Dokeos LMS
  • Localization readiness through Git and Crowdin
  • Feedback system for ideas, bug reports, and surveys

Process

01

Content Migration

I restructured existing MadCap Flare content into modular, topic-based learning units with clearer introductions, conclusions, and exercises.

02

Navigation Design

I designed breadcrumbs, tables of contents, menus, and context markers for both web and SCORM outputs.

03

SCORM Testing

I diagnosed and resolved SCORM tracking issues with Dokeos LMS using Flare settings and JavaScript API workarounds.

04

Feedback & Localization

I connected Microsoft Forms to Azure DevOps through Power Automate and structured the corpus for Crowdin localization with Git.

My Role

I led the end-to-end transformation from content strategy to technical implementation, including modular restructuring, visual alignment, output configuration, feedback workflows, and localization readiness.

Dual Output Strategy

The web output supports instructor-led training, either in person or remote, with flexible access to individual courses or complete sets. The SCORM output supports theoretical content delivery in Dokeos LMS and enables progress tracking.

This approach addressed current training needs while preparing the organization for a future transition toward fully LMS-driven learning.

Outcome

The project delivered a scalable, maintainable, and learner-friendly training system. The modules are easier to navigate, interactive, feedback-enabled, SCORM-ready, and aligned with corporate branding.

The system is also prepared for global localization, making it more sustainable for multilingual deployments.

What Changed

  • Training became modular instead of PDF-bound
  • Learners gained clearer paths and progress support
  • Feedback became actionable through Azure DevOps tickets
  • Localization became easier to plan and maintain
