About Me

Making complexity understandable

Learning Experience Designer & Developer

My work brings together learning design, technical communication, and product strategy. I turn complex technical concepts into clear, structured learning experiences that help people understand and use the products they work with.

Beyond individual resources, I build coherent learning paths and systems that make knowledge easier to access, maintain, and grow.

Approach

I start by understanding the product, the people using it, and the problems they need to solve. From there, I structure information and choose the formats that best support understanding, practice, and everyday use.

Accuracy, intellectual honesty, and critical thinking guide my decisions. I look for evidence, question assumptions, and pay attention to the details that make an explanation both clear and trustworthy.

I enjoy taking ownership, experimenting with tools, and finding practical ways to improve how work gets done. Automation interests me as a way to build sustainable systems and create more room for thoughtful design.

Interests

My curiosity extends into history, science, philosophy, literature, and technology. I enjoy exploring how things work and the reasoning behind the explanations we accept.

Photography gives me another way to explore perspective, detail, and visual storytelling. Across these interests, I care about both substance and presentation: making ideas accessible while preserving their depth.
