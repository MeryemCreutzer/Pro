Case Study

Internal Knowledge Management Optimization

A SharePoint transformation project that turned scattered internal documentation into a centralized, usable, and audit-ready knowledge hub.

A dormant SharePoint became a living knowledge base for documentation work.

The project reorganized internal documentation, modernized workflow tracking, and created clearer access to process, training, and management resources.

Client
PcVue
Type
Knowledge Management System
Role
Knowledge Manager
Published
April 23, 2025

Context

When I joined the company, SharePoint was technically available but functionally dormant. Documentation was fragmented across random links, outdated files, isolated Excel sheets, and siloed folders.

Essential processes were difficult to find and knowledge sharing was minimal, creating risks for documentation quality, team efficiency, and audit readiness.

Key Outcomes

  • Centralized scattered internal documentation
  • Streamlined access to process checklists with Microsoft Lists
  • Collected feedback with Microsoft Forms
  • Improved visibility of documentation and e-learning work
  • Contributed to ISO 9001 certification readiness

Process

01

Audit & Mapping

I audited existing documentation sources, identifying scattered, redundant, outdated, and missing process documentation.

02

Architecture Design

I designed a SharePoint structure around thematic areas such as onboarding, process management, review checklists, and related internal resources.

03

Reclassification & Labeling

I classified documents by type, confidentiality, and status, introducing a visual labeling system for lifecycle tracking and visibility.

04

Workflow Modernization

I used Figma, Microsoft Lists, and Microsoft Forms to modernize diagrams, checklists, feedback collection, and collaborative documentation workflows.

My Role

I took ownership of the transformation by conducting audits, mapping pain points, reorganizing the SharePoint space, and replacing static Excel-based workflows with more dynamic Microsoft Lists and Forms.

Solution

The optimized system brought together process documents, workflow diagrams, checklists, internal links, style guides, training materials, and feedback tools in one coherent knowledge space.

It also introduced clearer document ownership and made internal documentation easier to maintain over time.

Outcome

The optimized SharePoint space became a robust knowledge base supporting collaboration, training, audit readiness, and ongoing content quality.

It contributed to the company's ISO 9001 certification work by making content easier to access, structure, and maintain in alignment with process excellence standards.

What Changed

  • Less duplicated and scattered information
  • More reliable access to internal processes
  • Clearer workflow and review tracking
  • Better feedback and team engagement loops
