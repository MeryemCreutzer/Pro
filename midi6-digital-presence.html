Case Study

Building an Owner-Managed Website

A no-code website and Google Business Profile project for a guesthouse that needed a simple, maintainable online presence.

A small hospitality business needed a web presence the owner could actually own.

I created a Google Sites website connected to a real domain and supported it with a Google Business Profile so potential guests could discover the business and understand the offer.

Client
Midi 6
Role
No-Code Web Designer
Published
May 3, 2026

Context

Midi 6 is a guesthouse in Montmorin, near Billom, in the Parc naturel régional du Livradois-Forez. The business is positioned around calm, nature, ecological hospitality, and local discovery in Auvergne.

The owner needed a site they could understand, update, and maintain independently without relying on a developer for every small change.

Project Goals

  • Create a simple website with Google Sites
  • Connect the website to a custom domain
  • Make editing manageable for the owner
  • Improve discoverability through Google Business Profile
  • Help visitors understand rooms, location, values, and booking options

Process

01

Needs & Autonomy

I chose Google Sites because the owner needed a tool that was easy to learn, low-maintenance, and safe for independent updates.

02

Website Structure

I organized the site around practical visitor questions: welcome, rooms, location, surrounding activities, contact, and booking paths.

03

Domain & Visibility

I connected the website to a real domain and aligned the content with the business's ecological, calm, nature-focused positioning.

04

Google Business Profile

I supported the business profile setup so the owner could receive interaction data and understand how people engaged with the business through Google.

My Role

  • Designed and structured the Google Sites website
  • Helped connect the site to a custom domain
  • Created a web presence the owner could manage independently
  • Set up/support Google Business Profile visibility
  • Helped the owner access interaction and visibility statistics

Solution

The project combined a simple owner-editable website with a Google Business Profile. This gave the business a clearer public identity across direct visits, search, and local discovery moments.

The website gave visitors richer context about the guesthouse, while Google Business helped surface practical information and interaction data.

Outcome

The owner gained an online presence they could understand and update independently. The website is available through an actual domain, while Google Business visibility helped people discover the guesthouse and interact with it more easily.

The setup also gave the owner access to useful indicators, such as how people interacted with the website and business listing, supporting better awareness of online visibility and potential client interest.

What This Demonstrates

  • Choosing tools based on owner autonomy
  • Designing for maintainability, not just launch
  • Connecting website structure with local search visibility
  • Supporting small-business digital ownership
