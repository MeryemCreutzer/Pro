Case Study

Content Architecture Solution Project

A structured content architecture designed for RobotsMali teachers, turning scattered educational materials into a centralized, navigable, and scalable resource system.

CASP made teaching resources easier to find, understand, and reuse.

The project introduced a modular hierarchy, interactive guides, and a help center so teachers could move through instructional materials with less friction.

Client
RobotsMali
Role
Information Architect & Project Manager
Published
March 21, 2025

Context

RobotsMali is a STEM and robotics education program supporting students and educators. Over time, the organization accumulated lesson plans and instructional guides across scattered locations. Teachers had to spend time searching, guessing, and reconstructing paths to the right materials.

CASP was created to replace that scattered experience with a centralized, hierarchical, and intuitive content management framework.

Skills & Expertise

  • User research & requirements analysis
  • Content architecture & taxonomy design
  • Google Drive & Google Sites
  • Wireframing & usability testing
  • XMind & Adobe XD
  • Functional specifications & user guides

Process

01

User Research & Requirements

We analyzed how teachers accessed existing resources and identified workflow problems such as scattered content, unclear file structures, and repeated manual steps.

02

System Architecture

We designed a modular Google Drive and Google Sites hierarchy that mirrored how educational content was delivered.

03

Prototyping & Testing

Paper mockups, XMind maps, Adobe XD wireframes, Genially diagrams, and Canva visuals helped test and refine navigation patterns.

04

Deployment & Documentation

We delivered a help center, user guides, functional specifications, and structural specifications to support adoption and future maintenance.

My Role

  • Led communication between sponsor and development team
  • Designed the system architecture and navigation framework
  • Created functional specifications and user guides
  • Tested and refined the interface with UX principles

Outcomes

CASP brought scattered teaching materials into a structured resource hub:

  • Centralized educational resources in a Google Drive and Google Sites framework.
  • Organized content into sections, classes, and subclasses with search and filtering.
  • Created interactive guides to help teachers navigate learning materials.
  • Delivered user guides and functional and structural specifications to support adoption and maintenance.
